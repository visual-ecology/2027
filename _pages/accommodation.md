## Accommodation in Oxford

### Accommodation at Magdalen

We have reserved **50 rooms at Magdalen College** for conference attendees, available to book on a **first-come, first-served basis**.

These rooms are normally used as student accommodation during term time and offer single occupancy with shared bathroom facilities. All bookings include a **breakfast buffet served each morning in Magdalen College's historic medieval dining hall**.

Rooms can be booked directly through the college's accommodation booking system using the button below.

**Please note:** Accommodation bookings are made directly with Magdalen College and are managed independently of the conference organisers. For any questions, special requests or concerns regarding your booking, please contact the college directly.


### Accommodation at other colleges

As the conference takes place outside the University of Oxford's academic term dates, other Oxford colleges may also offer their student accommodation to visitors as short-term bed and breakfast (B&B) packages.

It is therefore worth keeping an eye on accommodation availability at other colleges, particularly those within walking distance of Magdalen College. Availability and booking arrangements vary between colleges, so we recommend checking their websites directly for up-to-date information.


### Hotels and private accommodation

Thanks to Magdalen College's central location at the eastern end of Oxford's High Street, numerous hotels, guesthouses and other accommodation options are within walking distance of the conference venue or can be easily reached by public transport.

Attendees may also wish to explore private accommodation options, including rooms and apartments available through Airbnb. We recommend booking early, as accommodation in Oxford can be in high demand during the summer months.
