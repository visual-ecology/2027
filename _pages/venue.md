## Getting there

The Grove Auditorium can be accessed via the college's Wall Gate entrance on Longwall Street.

**By car:** Parking is not available on college premises, and nearby on-street parking is limited and expensive. Attendees travelling by car are therefore encouraged to use one of Oxford's [Park & Ride facilities](https://www.oxford.gov.uk/park-and-ride), particularly Redbridge Park & Ride, or to park outside the city and continue their journey by public transport. However, **visitors with mobility or accessibility requirements** may be eligible for special parking arrangements on college grounds, subject to prior approval. If you require accessible parking, please contact Christian Drerup (christian.drerup [at] magd.ox.ac.uk) in advance to discuss possible arrangements.

**By public transport:** Magdalen College is located at the eastern end of Oxford's High Street and is well served by local bus routes, with several stops within walking distance of the conference venue. For routes and timetables, please consult the [Oxford Bus Company](https://www.oxfordbus.co.uk/). **Oxford Railway Station** is approximately a 20–25-minute walk from the college, or around 15 minutes by bus, depending on the service. Frequent train connections are available from London Paddington and other major UK cities. Visitors travelling from London can also use the [Oxford Tube](https://www.oxfordtube.com/), a frequent coach service connecting central London with Oxford.

**For international attendees:** Oxford does not have a major international airport, but is accessible from several UK airports, including London Heathrow, Gatwick, Stansted and Luton, as well as Birmingham and Bristol. Direct coach connections to Oxford are available from Heathrow and Gatwick via [the Airline](https://www.theairlineoxford.co.uk/), while other airports can be reached using combinations of train and coach services. Alternatively, visitors travelling from mainland Europe may consider the [Eurostar](https://www.eurostar.com/uk-en) to London St Pancras International, from where onward connections to Oxford are available by train or coach.
