> {{ site.data.conference.name }} aims to be a welcoming, safe and productive meeting for
> everyone, regardless of career stage, gender, gender identity and expression, sexual
> orientation, disability, physical appearance, ethnicity, nationality, religion, age or any
> other personal characteristic. This code of conduct applies to all attendees, speakers,
> organisers, volunteers and sponsors, at every conference event — talks, poster sessions,
> social events, field trips and online spaces.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate, in person and online.
- Engage constructively with ideas and give credit to others' work.
- Respect people's boundaries, and requests not to be photographed or recorded.
- Look out for one another, and let the organisers know if someone needs help.
- Do not photograph or record slides or posters that are labelled with a 'no social media' logo, and ask for permission if unsure:

![No social media](/assets/logos/no-social-media.png)

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation or discrimination in any form.
- Offensive comments related to any of the personal characteristics listed above.
- Unwelcome physical contact or sexual attention.
- Sustained disruption of talks or other events.
- Stalking, following, or deliberate intimidation.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern.

## Reporting a concern

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaks this code of conduct, please contact
**{{ contact.name }}** ({{ contact.email }}) or any member of the
[organising committee]({{ '/organisers/' | relative_url }}). All reports will be treated
confidentially, and you do not need to have been directly affected to make one.

If you or anyone else is in immediate danger, contact the local emergency services first.

## Consequences

Anyone asked to stop unacceptable behaviour is expected to comply immediately. The
organisers may take any action they consider appropriate, including a warning or removal
from the conference without a refund, and may inform the person's home institution or the
relevant authorities.
